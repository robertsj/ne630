## Problem 3 [Lewis Eqs. (1.21)--(1.23)]

An idealized prompt-neutron chain begins with $n_0$ neutrons. Each neutron generation takes an average time $\ell$, and the multiplication factor $k$ is constant.

- **(a)** Beginning with $n_{i+1}=kn_i$, derive

  $$
  n(t)=n_0 k^{t/\ell}.
  $$

- **(b)** For $k\approx 1$, show that

  $$
  n(t)\approx n_0\exp\left(\frac{k-1}{\ell}t\right).
  $$

- **(c)** Classify systems having $k=0.995$, $k=1.000$, and $k=1.005$ as subcritical, critical, or supercritical, and describe the long-term behavior of the neutron population in each system.
- **(d)** A reactor initially produces $1.25~\mathrm{MW(t)}$, with $k=1.010$ and $\ell=10^{-5}~\mathrm{s}$. Assuming $P(t)\propto n(t)$, estimate the time required to reach $2.00~\mathrm{MW(t)}$ using both the exact and approximate expressions.
