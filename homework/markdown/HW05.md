- Uranium makes up 8.5% of the mixture **by mass**.
- The uranium is enriched to 20% ${}^{235}\mathrm{U}$ **by atom**; assume the balance is ${}^{238}\mathrm{U}$.
- Natural zirconium consists of ${}^{90}\mathrm{Zr}$ (51.5%), ${}^{91}\mathrm{Zr}$ (11.2%), ${}^{92}\mathrm{Zr}$ (17.1%), ${}^{94}\mathrm{Zr}$ (17.4%), and ${}^{96}\mathrm{Zr}$ (2.8%), all by atom.

  M_{\mathrm{ZrH}_{1.6}}
    =\overline{M}_{\mathrm{Zr}}+1.6(1),\\
  n_{\mathrm{ZrH}_{1.6}}
    =\frac{(0.915)(7.1)N_A}{M_{\mathrm{ZrH}_{1.6}}},\\

    =1.6n_{\mathrm{ZrH}_{1.6}},\\

    =0.515n_{\mathrm{ZrH}_{1.6}},\\[3pt]

    =0.20(235)+0.80(238),\\

  n_{{}^{235}\mathrm{U}}
    =0.20n_{\mathrm{U}},\\
  n_{{}^{238}\mathrm{U}}
    =0.80n_{\mathrm{U}}.
  \end{gathered}
  $$
