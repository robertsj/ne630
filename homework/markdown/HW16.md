## Problem 1 [FNRP 4.3]

A sodium-cooled fast reactor is fueled with PuO$_2$ mixed with depleted UO$_2$; the structural material is iron. The following microscopic cross sections are averaged over the fast-neutron spectrum.

| Material | $\sigma_f$ (b) | $\sigma_a$ (b) | $\sigma_t$ (b) | $\rho$ ($\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PuO$_2$ | 1.95 | 2.40 | 8.6 | 11.0 |
| UO$_2$ | 0.05 | 0.404 | 8.2 | 11.0 |
| Na | -- | 0.0018 | 3.7 | 0.97 |
| Fe | -- | 0.0087 | 3.6 | 7.87 |

The fuel is 15% PuO$_2$ and 85% UO$_2$ by volume. The core is 30% fuel, 50% coolant, and 20% structural material by volume.

- **(a)** Calculate $k_\infty$, taking $\nu=2.98$ for plutonium and $\nu=2.47$ for uranium in the fast spectrum and neglecting the cross sections of oxygen.
- **(b)** What fraction of the core mass is fuel?

## Problem 2 [FNRP 4.4]

What volume percent of the fuel should be PuO$_2$?
