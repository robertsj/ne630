## Problem 1 [FNRP 4.6]

A pressurized water reactor has 3 atom-% enriched UO$_2$ fuel pins with diameter $D=1.0~\mathrm{cm}$ and density $11.0~\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$. The moderator-to-fuel volume ratio is 2:1. At room temperature, calculate $\eta_T$, $p$, $f$, and $k_\infty$ under the assumptions that

- $\epsilon=1.24$,
- the thermal disadvantage factor is $\varsigma=1.16$, and
- the Dancoff correction is represented by increasing the fuel diameter by 10% in the resonance-integral calculation.

Use the fuel data from FNRP Problem 3.4 (used in Homework 09, Problem 2) and the approximate expression for $p$ in FNRP Eq. (4.40), with Table 4.3.

## Problem 2 [FNRP 4.8]

A reactor lattice consists of uranium rods in heavy water. The heavy water is replaced by light water.

- **(a)** Would the resonance escape probability increase or decrease? Why?
- **(b)** Would the thermal utilization increase or decrease? Why?
- **(c)** What would you expect the net effect on $k_\infty$ to be? Why?

## Problem 3 [FNRP 4.9]

Suppose the coolant-to-fuel volume ratio is increased in a pressurized water reactor.

- **(a)** Will the fast-fission factor increase, decrease, or remain unchanged? Why?
- **(b)** Will the resonance escape probability increase, decrease, or remain unchanged? Why?
