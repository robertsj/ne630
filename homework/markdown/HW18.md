## Problem 2

Use the same PWR pin-cell model at nominal conditions, $T_f=1200~\mathrm{K}$, $T_m=600~\mathrm{K}$, and $P=15.5~\mathrm{MPa}$. Compute the four factors and $k_\infty$ as functions of pitch-to-diameter ratio over $1.1\le P/D\le3.0$. Produce

- **(a)** a plot of $k_\infty$ versus $P/D$, and
- **(b)** one plot containing $\ln\eta$, $\ln f$, $\ln p$, and $\ln\epsilon$ versus $P/D$.

The second plot converts the product $k_\infty=\eta f p\epsilon$ to the sum

$$
\ln k_\infty=\ln\eta+\ln f+\ln p+\ln\epsilon,
$$

making each factor's contribution visible. Use enough $P/D$ values to obtain smooth curves comparable to FNRP Figure 4.7.
