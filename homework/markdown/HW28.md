## Problem 2

Consider a critical reactor with $n(0)=10^6~\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$ subject to
a step insertion of reactivity $\rho_0$ at $t=0$. Using the kinetics
equations with a single precursor group, i.e.,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dn}{dt}&=\frac{\rho(t)-\beta}{\Lambda}n(t)+\lambda c(t),\\
\frac{dc}{dt}&=-\lambda c(t)+\frac{\beta}{\Lambda}n(t),
\end{aligned}
$$

compute $n(t)$ and $c(t)$ at $t=0$, $t=0.1$, and $t=10~\mathrm{s}$
assuming $\beta=0.0065$, $\lambda=0.08~\mathrm{s^{-1}}$,
$\Lambda=5\times10^{-5}~\mathrm{s}$, and

- **(a)** $\rho_0=0.2\beta$
- **(b)** $\rho_0=-0.2\beta$

Make sure to clearly indicate intermediate results, e.g., the time
constants, coefficients, etc.

## Problem 3 

Consider again the kinetics equations with a single precursor group (as
defined in Problem 2). Suppose a step reactivity $\rho$ is inserted into
an initially critical reactor. Show that if

$$
\frac{1}{\lambda\Lambda}|\beta-\rho|\gg1
\quad\text{and}\quad
\frac{\beta}{\lambda\Lambda}\gg1,
$$

then

$$
n(t)=n(0)\left(
\frac{\rho}{\rho-\beta}e^{\frac{\rho-\beta}{\Lambda}t}
+\frac{\beta}{\beta-\rho}e^{\frac{\lambda\rho}{\beta-\rho}t}
\right).
$$
