\noindent{\bf Problem 1 [FNRP 4.3].}

A sodium-cooled fast reactor contains 15 vol\% PuO$_2$ and 85 vol\%
depleted UO$_2$ within its fuel. The core is 30 vol\% fuel, 50 vol\% sodium,
and 20 vol\% iron. Using the stated fast-spectrum data, determine
$k_\infty$ and the fuel mass fraction.

\begin{adaptedsolution}
Neglect oxygen reactions as directed and use one heavy-metal atom per oxide
molecule. The fuel-averaged production and absorption cross sections are
\begin{align*}
\nu\bar\Sigma_f^f
&=0.15\frac{(11)N_A}{239+32}(2.98)(1.95\times10^{-24})
 +0.85\frac{(11)N_A}{238+32}(2.47)(0.05\times10^{-24})\\
&=0.02388~\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\\
\bar\Sigma_a^f
&=0.15\frac{(11)N_A}{239+32}(2.40\times10^{-24})
 +0.85\frac{(11)N_A}{238+32}(0.404\times10^{-24})\\
&=0.01722~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}.
\end{align*}
For sodium and iron,
\begin{align*}
\bar\Sigma_a^c
&=\frac{(0.97)N_A}{22.99}(0.0018\times10^{-24})
=4.573\times10^{-5}~\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\\
\bar\Sigma_a^{st}
&=\frac{(7.87)N_A}{55.85}(0.0087\times10^{-24})
=7.383\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}.
\end{align*}
Thus
\[
k_\infty=
\frac{0.30\nu\bar\Sigma_f^f}
{0.30\bar\Sigma_a^f+0.50\bar\Sigma_a^c+0.20\bar\Sigma_a^{st}}
=\boxed{1.342}.
\]
The density of the fuel mixture remains $11.0~\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$ because
both oxide components have that density. Therefore
\[
w_f=\frac{0.30(11.0)}{0.30(11.0)+0.50(0.97)+0.20(7.87)}
=\boxed{0.616\ \text{(61.6\%)}}.
\]
\end{adaptedsolution}

\noindent{\bf Problem 2 [FNRP 4.4].}

For the reactor in Problem 1, take $P_{\mathrm{NL}}=0.90$ and find the
PuO$_2$ fraction within the fuel that gives $k_{\mathrm{eff}}=1$.

\begin{adaptedsolution}
Let $x=V_{\mathrm{PuO_2}}/V_f$; then $1-x$ is the UO$_2$ fraction. From the
component calculations in Problem 1,
\[
\nu\Sigma_f^f(x)=0.14204x+0.00303(1-x),
\qquad
\Sigma_a^f(x)=0.05866x+0.00991(1-x).
\]
Keeping the core region fractions fixed gives
\[
1=k_{\mathrm{eff}}
=0.90\frac{0.30[0.14204x+0.00303(1-x)]}
{0.30[0.05866x+0.00991(1-x)]
 +0.50(4.573\times10^{-5})+0.20(7.383\times10^{-4})}.
\]
Collecting terms,
\[
1=\frac{0.03753x+0.00082}{0.01463x+0.00314},
\qquad
x=\boxed{0.101\ \text{(about 10.1 vol\% PuO$_2$ in the fuel)}}.
\]
Substitution returns $k_{\mathrm{eff}}=1.00$ to the precision of the input
data.
\end{adaptedsolution}
