# Learning objectives implied by FNRP Chapters 1–4

Each table lists the objectives a student must meet to work the chapter's
reading-guide frames, the frames that exercise them, and the closest existing
course objective (the micro objectives in `pages/lesson_NN.md`).

Status key:

- **covered** — an existing lesson objective says essentially the same thing.
- **partial** — an existing objective touches it but is narrower or vaguer.
- **NEW** — no existing lesson objective; flagged for possible inclusion.
- **course-only** — a lesson objective with no counterpart in Lewis's text;
  the guide supplies the development.

Frame numbers refer to the compiled guides (`ch01_guide.pdf`, etc.).

> The Chapter 6 and 7 guides also point to this file. Their comparison tables
> have not been written yet; only Chapters 1–4 are tabulated here.

### Lesson objectives the guides do not exercise

These are computational or tool objectives that a paper guide cannot carry:

- L5: find $\sigma_x(E)$ from a tabulation; plot tabulated cross sections.
- L6: access and inspect cross sections with OpenMC.
- L12: numerically compute an effective cross section from tabulated data.
- L13: use OpenMC to produce multigroup cross sections.
- L14: plot multigroup functions versus energy and lethargy.
- L18: Beocat access and OpenMC installation.
- L19: fit data points to a polynomial; run the OpenMC $P/D$ and Pu-fraction scans.

### Flagged for possible inclusion

1. **Fuel fissioned per unit energy** (1.9): one division, and it anchors every
   later burnup discussion.
2. **Time scale of a prompt chain** (1.11): motivates Chapter 5 weeks early.
3. **Wigner–Way decay heat** (1.13): the lesson currently quotes only 6%.
4. **First-collision density and mean free path** (2.4) and **cross section
   from transmission** (2.3): already taught in Lesson 4, missing from its list.
5. **Compound-nucleus excitation energy** (2.12): explains why thermal neutrons
   can fission U-235 but not U-238.
6. **Group-to-group scattering conventions and conservation** (3.19, 3.22) and
   **source multiplication** (3.26): taught in Lessons 13–14, missing from
   their lists.
7. **Two-group $k_\infty$ decomposition** (3.24): a natural bridge to Chapter 4.
8. **Core sizing from $q'$ and $\bar P'''$** (4.2): two divisions that make
   Table 4.1 concrete.
9. **Mean free path versus pin size** (4.6): the test that separates fast from
   thermal lattice treatments; taught in Lesson 16 but not listed.
10. **The exponential form of $p$, the empirical resonance integral, and a
    complete hand calculation of $k_\infty$** (4.12–4.14): Lesson 18's
    published objectives are all about Beocat and OpenMC setup, so the
    chapter's central calculation has no listed objective.
11. **Boron and the optimum moderation** (4.16): explains why a lattice can
    pass from under- to overmoderated without any change of geometry.
